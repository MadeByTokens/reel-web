How Reel works

        
      

      

        
        

          
Adding things

          
          
    
            
  • Media adds videos and pictures to the main track, one after another.
    • 
            
  • Layer puts a picture, a video, Text or a Sticker on top of the main track, starting at the playhead.
    • 
            
  • Audio adds music or sounds below. Voice records with the microphone.
    • 
          

        

        

          
The timeline

          
          
    
            
  • Drag the timeline left and right. The line in the middle is the playhead; the preview shows that moment.
    • 
            
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom the timeline in and out.
    • 
            
  • Tap a clip to select it. Drag the handles at its ends to trim it.
    • 
            
  • Press and hold a clip, then drag, to move it or change the order.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Cutting and joining

          
          
    
            
  • Trim/Split cuts the selected clip at the playhead, or removes the part before or after it.
    • 
            
  • The small square between two main-track clips sets the Transition from one to the next.
    • 
            
  • Layers and sounds stay with the main-track clip they sit over: when that clip moves, they move too.
    • 
          

        

        

          
The preview

          
          
    
            
  • Select a layer, then drag it in the preview to move it. Drag the corner arrow to resize and the round arrow to turn. Two fingers do both at once.
    • 
            
  • Tap Play to watch. Tap again to stop.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Effects

          
          
    
            
  • Keyframes make a layer move: add one, move the playhead, then move, resize or turn the layer. It glides between the two.
    • 
            
  • Chroma Key removes one colour from a layer, such as a green background.
    • 
            
  • Color Filter gives a clip a ready-made look; Adjustment changes brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Finishing

          
          
    
            
  • Export makes a video file. Keep Reel open and on screen until it finishes, then tap Save.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Keeping your work

          
          
    
            
  • Everything is saved on this device as you edit. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    • 
            
  • Add Reel to the Home Screen so the browser never clears your projects.
    • 
            
  • Save project file in the menu makes a backup, with its videos, that you can keep in Files. Open it again from the projects screen.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Keys

          
          
    
            
  • Space plays, arrows step one frame, S splits, Delete removes the selected clip, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo.
    • 
            
  • Press and hold any button to see what it does.
